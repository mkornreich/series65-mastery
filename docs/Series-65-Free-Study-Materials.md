# Best Free Series 65 Study Materials

A skeptical review of the **26 study-material vendors listed by NASAA**
([nasaa.org/exams/study-material-vendors](https://www.nasaa.org/exams/study-material-vendors/)),
scored by what a candidate can actually access **without paying**. NASAA itself
"does not require, approve, endorse, or recommend any study/preparation materials."

Each vendor was researched and the top candidates were independently verified by
loading their live pages. "Free" here means no payment — a page that needs only an
email is noted; a "free trial" that needs a credit card or unlocks a paid course
is **not** counted as free.

_Reviewed August 2026 — vendor offerings change, so confirm on the linked pages._

---

## The two winners

Only two vendors give a **genuinely free, full-length 130-question practice exam
with per-question explanations and no signup**. Everyone else caps free practice at
~10–15 questions or gates it behind a trial or purchase.

### 🥇 Best overall free package — Capital Advantage Tutoring (Ken Finnen / "Series 7 Whisperer")
- **Free 130-question Series 65 exam** with an explanation under every answer
  ("Ken's Take"), optional 3-hour timed mode, **no signup or email at all**.
  → https://series7exam.org/free-series-65-practice-exam/
- Plus the largest *free instruction* of any vendor: **hundreds of free NASAA video
  lessons on YouTube** and a **free live weekly NASAA Q&A**. Best choice if you want
  to actually learn for free.
- Caveat: a small one-person operation (not a polished company); the site's nav
  upsells paid tutoring/a book, but none of that gates the free exam.

### 🥇 Best free practice exam + reference — Achievable
- **Free 130-question Series 65 exam**, instant scoring, detailed explanations,
  **no signup or credit card**.
  → https://achievable.me/exams/finra-series-65/free-practice-exam/
- Plus free downloadable **cheat / "dump" sheets** (formula & reference PDFs, no
  login) and a free FINRA podcast.
- The most polished, established option. The textbook, extra exams, and 50+ videos
  are in the paid ($199) course.

**Recommendation:** use **both** — Achievable for the polished free full-length exam
plus cheat sheets, and Capital Advantage (Ken Finnen) for a second free full exam
plus free video teaching and live Q&A.
